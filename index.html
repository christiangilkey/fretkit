Fretkit ·

Questions, privacy requests or a problem to report:

Everything you make in Fretkit (songs, grooves, lesson progress and settings) is saved only on this device. There is no account, and no ads, analytics or tracking. Chord shapes are worked out for whatever instrument, tuning and capo you choose, so alternate tunings get real fingerings too.

All riffs, exercises, example songs and lyrics in Fretkit are original. Artist names, song titles and gear names are used only to describe styles and sounds. Product names and trademarks belong to their owners, and their use does not imply any endorsement or affiliation.
