Privacy Policy

Fretkit · Last updated November 9, 2026

In short: everything you make in Fretkit stays on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and there are no ads, analytics, tracking or crash reports. The Android app has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere. We never receive your songs, lyrics, settings or the sound from your microphone.

Who we are

Fretkit is provided by Caliber Products LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Fretkit Android app and the Fretkit web app at christiangilkey.github.io/fretkit. Questions and privacy requests: tamkeen.app.help@gmail.com.

What Fretkit keeps on your device

To work, Fretkit saves these on your device, and nowhere else:

  • your songs: titles, sections, chords, lyrics, tempo and key, and the syllable stresses you set;
  • drum grooves you make or copy;
  • your lesson progress and the song you are building in each course;
  • your settings: instrument, tuning, capo, left-handed layout, note names and display choices;
  • small reminders, such as the page you had open and which version of these documents you agreed to.

In the Android app this is in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. In the web app it is in your browser's storage for the website's address. None of it is sent to us. You can erase all of it at any time in Setup → About → Erase all Fretkit data, by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the website's data in your browser's settings.

The microphone (tuner)

Fretkit uses the microphone only for the tuner, only after you tap Start tuner, and only while the tuner is listening. It turns off when you tap Stop tuner or leave the tuner. The sound is analysed on your device, a fraction of a second at a time, to find the pitch of the string you play. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Your phone or browser asks your permission the first time; if you say no, everything else in Fretkit still works, and you can change your answer in your phone's or browser's settings.

Copying and restoring songs

Copy all songs and Copy as text put a text copy of your songs on your device's clipboard, and you decide where to paste it. Other apps that can read your clipboard may be able to see it, so paste it somewhere you trust. Restore from backup reads only text you paste in yourself, checks it, and adds only valid songs and grooves.

Android's own backup

If backup to your Google account is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Fretkit's saved songs and settings in that backup, stored in your Google account under Google's terms. We can't see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Links to other websites

Lessons link to their sources and to further reading, and Listen opens a YouTube search for the song. These open in your browser, and the website you visit (for example YouTube, which belongs to Google) handles your visit under its own privacy policy. Fretkit doesn't tell them which page of Fretkit you came from.

The website

The web app is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When your browser loads it, GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep it in its logs for security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fretkit's pages load nothing from any other company: the fonts and icons are served with the app.

Inside Claude

If someone shares Fretkit with you as a page on claude.ai, it runs inside Anthropic's Claude service, and your songs may be saved to your own Claude account so they appear on your other devices. That is handled by Anthropic under its own terms and privacy policy, and we can't see those songs.

Analytics, advertising and selling data

Fretkit has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools, and no code from other companies that collects data. We don't sell or share personal data, build profiles of people, or make automated decisions about them.

Children

Fretkit has no accounts, chat or ways to contact other people, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It can be used at any age. A parent can erase everything Fretkit has saved at any time (see above).

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, it is already in your hands: you can see, change, copy and delete it in the app. We hold no personal data about you on any server of ours. You can still email tamkeen.app.help@gmail.com with any privacy question or request, and we answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Why data is used (EU and UK). Fretkit stores your data on your device only to give you the features you use (performance of our agreement with you). GitHub's technical logs for the website serve its security (legitimate interest).

Where and how long. Your data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. GitHub, a US company, keeps its website logs for as long as its own policy says.

Do Not Track. Fretkit doesn't track you, so it works the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Security

Fretkit loads no code from the internet, checks everything you restore before using it, and shows text from backups only as plain text, never as code. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your device locked and keep a backup copy of songs that matter to you.

Changes and contact

If Fretkit ever starts storing or sending data in a new way, we will update this policy first, in the same release, and tell you in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version. Contact: Caliber Products LLC, Florida, USA · tamkeen.app.help@gmail.com.
