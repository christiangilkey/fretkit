Cookie and Storage Notice

Fretkit · Effective November 9, 2026

In short: Fretkit sets no cookies and uses no advertising, analytics or tracking. It keeps your data in your device's storage only so the app works. Fretkit is provided by Caliber Products LLC; see also the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What "cookies and storage" means

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Apps and web apps can also save data on your device in other ways, such as the browser's local storage. The law treats these the same way, so this notice covers all of them.

Storage Fretkit needs to work

These are strictly necessary for features you use, so they don't need your consent. They stay on your device and are never used to track you or show ads.

  • Your songs and grooves: everything you write in the Song, Lyrics and Drums sections.
  • Lesson progress: which steps you have finished and the song you are building in each course.
  • Settings: instrument, tuning, capo, left-handed layout, note names and display choices.
  • Small reminders: the page you had open, and which version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you agreed to, and when.

The Android app keeps these in its private storage. The web app keeps them in your browser's local storage for the address christiangilkey.github.io, which also hosts other apps by the same developer.

Cookies from other services

Fretkit shows no other company's content inside the app. When you open a link (for example a lesson's source, or a YouTube search from Listen), it opens in your browser, and that website may set its own cookies under its own policy.

Your choices

  • To remove everything Fretkit keeps on a device, use Setup → About → Erase all Fretkit data, or uninstall the app. In a browser, you can also clear the website's data in the browser's settings.
  • Blocking the storage Fretkit needs will stop it from remembering your songs and settings.

Changes and contact

If Fretkit ever starts using cookies or storage that aren't strictly necessary, such as analytics, we will update this notice first and ask for your consent where the law requires it. Questions: tamkeen.app.help@gmail.com.
